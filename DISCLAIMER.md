# Project Disclaimer
SMITAH is a conceptual proposal for a creative well-being platform. This repository does not contain a working application. Feature descriptions, user journeys, quiz questions and diagrams are proposed design material, not evidence that features exist or have been tested.

SMITAH is not a medical, psychological, diagnostic, counselling or emergency service. Any future professional-support feature would require clearly defined qualifications, safeguarding, privacy and referral arrangements.

Do not claim improved happiness, reduced stress or improved mental health without appropriate research and evidence.
