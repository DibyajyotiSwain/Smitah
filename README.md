# SMITAH — Crafting A Glimpse of Smile

**SMITAH is inspired by the Sanskrit word _Smita (स्मित)_, meaning “smile.”**

SMITAH is a proposed creative well-being platform concept exploring how self-discovery, creative expression, mindfulness, skill-building and positive community experiences could be brought together in one user-centred experience.

> **Crafting A Glimpse of Smile.**

**Project stage:** Concept proposal and academic documentation  

---

## The four pillars

The original SMITAH concept is organised around four pillars. These remain the foundation of the project.

| Pillar | Main idea | Original feature areas |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Create Your Profile** | Build a personal identity and shape the experience | Craft your identity; personalise your interface; explore yourself through customisation |
| **2. Main Features** | Discover creative and reflective activities | Creative dashboard; real-time creative prompts; meditation; skill builder; community challenges |
| **3. Unleashing Uniqueness** | Explore, connect and express yourself | Virtual travel adventures; online meetings with healers; sharing daily moments |
| **4. Refining Ourselves** | Improve the experience through feedback | Regular updates; flexible user suggestions; well-being platform |

The supporting documents explain how these pillars could work together. They do not replace the original concept.

## Project status and evidence

SMITAH is currently a **concept-stage proposal**. The storyboard, quiz, flowcharts, sample interactions and feature descriptions represent proposed designs. They are not screenshots or evidence of a working application.

Expected outcomes must not be described as proven results. No user testing, clinical evaluation or measured improvement in happiness or mental health is claimed in this repository. Research claims should be supported with traceable sources recorded in `research/README.md`.
