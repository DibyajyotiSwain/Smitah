# 7. Expected Outcomes and Evaluation

## Expected outcomes
These are intended outcomes, not measured results.
- Easier discovery of creative activities.
- Opportunities for self-expression through prompts and challenges.
- A structured place to explore mindfulness activities.
- Greater visibility of personal interests and preferences.
- Opportunities for respectful community participation.
- A feedback process for future improvements.

## Possible evaluation plan
If the concept is later tested, conduct an appropriate usability study with informed participants.

| Area | Possible measure |
|---|---|
| Navigation | Task completion and observed difficulties |
| Clarity | User rating of feature descriptions |
| Personalisation | Whether users can understand and change preferences |
| Activity relevance | Optional rating of suggested activities |
| Accessibility | Review against selected accessibility criteria |
| Community safety | Review of privacy and moderation concepts |
| Usability | Structured user feedback |

Do not report improved happiness, reduced stress or improved mental health unless an appropriate study has been conducted and the evidence supports that conclusion.
