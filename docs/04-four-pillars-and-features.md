# 4. Four Main Pillars and Proposed Features

This section preserves the original SMITAH flowchart.

## 4.1 Create Your Profile
**Original points:** Craft your identity; personalise your own interface; explore yourself through customisation.

**Explanation:** Users create a profile and may select interests, preferred activity types and interface choices. Preferences should remain optional and editable.

**Examples:** choose interests such as drawing, writing, music, nature or learning; select a visual theme; choose preferred activity duration.

## 4.2 Main Features
**Original points:** Creative dashboard with numerous activities; real-time creative prompts; meditation, skill builder and community challenges.

**Explanation:** The dashboard is the central discovery area. It could present activity categories, suggested prompts and optional challenges. Users choose what they want to explore.

**Examples:** daily drawing or writing prompt; short breathing activity; beginner skill task; community creativity challenge.

## 4.3 Unleashing Uniqueness
**Original points:** Virtual travel adventures; online meetings with the healers; sharing daily moments.

**Explanation:** This pillar extends the experience into exploration, connection and self-expression. Virtual travel could introduce places, cultures or themed experiences. Healer sessions would require clearly defined qualifications, safeguarding and service boundaries. Daily moments could be shared with privacy controls.

## 4.4 Refining Ourselves
**Original points:** Regular updates for improvement; flexible user suggestions; well-being platform.

**Explanation:** Optional ratings, suggestion forms and usability reviews could inform future improvements. Updates should be prioritised around user needs, accessibility and safety.

## How the pillars connect
The profile captures preferences. The dashboard presents activities. Unleashing Uniqueness adds exploration and community experiences. Refining Ourselves gathers feedback and informs future improvements. The experience is iterative, not a one-way sequence.
