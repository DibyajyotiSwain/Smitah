# 3. Target Users and Personas

## Proposed audience
People interested in creative activities, personal discovery, mindfulness, learning new skills and positive social interaction. The final age range and accessibility requirements should be established through research.

## Illustrative personas
These are fictional design tools, not research participants.

### The Curious Explorer
Wants to discover interests and try new activities. Potential need: varied prompts and a personalised starting point.

### The Creative Beginner
Wants to express ideas but may not know where to start. Potential need: approachable prompts and beginner-friendly challenges.

### The Mindful Learner
Wants optional reflective activities in a daily routine. Potential need: simple mindfulness activities and reflection.

### The Community Contributor
Enjoys sharing work and learning with others. Potential need: respectful community spaces and clear sharing controls.

## Research questions
- Which activities do intended users find engaging?
- What does personalisation mean to them?
- How comfortable are they sharing creative work?
- What makes a challenge motivating rather than stressful?
- Which accessibility and privacy controls matter?
