# 2. Problem Statement and Objectives

## Problem context
The proposal responds to a design opportunity: people may want a single, approachable digital space combining creative self-expression, personal discovery, mindfulness and positive community participation. These are design assumptions to validate through research, not claims about every user.

Potential needs include finding relevant activities, getting started with creative prompts, exploring skills without pressure, accessing optional reflection activities and sharing work safely.

## Problem statement
How might a personalised digital platform bring together creative expression, self-discovery, mindfulness, skill-building and community participation in a clear and engaging experience that users can adapt to their interests?

## Primary objective
To develop a structured conceptual design for SMITAH around its four original pillars.

## Specific objectives
1. Define intended users and potential needs.
2. Propose a profile and personalisation experience.
3. Design a creative dashboard concept with activity prompts.
4. Describe mindfulness, skill-building and community challenges.
5. Explore virtual travel, online healer meetings and daily-moment sharing as proposed experiences.
6. Define a feedback and refinement cycle.
7. Represent the concept through storyboards, flowcharts and sample boards.
8. Identify limitations and a responsible evaluation plan.

## Scope
**Included:** concept definition, feature explanations, personas, sample quiz, storyboards, flowcharts, methodology and expected outcomes.

**Not included:** software development, live accounts, real-time recommendation engines, clinical assessment, therapy services or measured impact claims.
