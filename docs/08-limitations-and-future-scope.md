# 8. Limitations and Future Scope

## Current limitations
- SMITAH is a concept and has not been implemented.
- Personas and sample journeys are illustrative until validated.
- Personalisation has not been tested.
- Virtual travel and online healer meetings need clearer service definitions.
- Community features require moderation, privacy and safeguarding policies.
- No measured well-being outcomes are available.

## Future scope
1. Clickable prototype for usability testing.
2. Accessibility and inclusive-design review.
3. Privacy-conscious preference and recommendation model.
4. Curated creative learning content.
5. Carefully moderated community spaces.
6. Qualification and safeguarding standards for facilitator sessions.
7. Partnerships with qualified well-being and creative-learning organisations.
8. Pilot study with informed consent and appropriate evaluation.

## Responsible boundaries
SMITAH is a creative well-being concept, not a therapy service, diagnostic tool or emergency support service. Any future professional support feature needs qualified providers, safeguarding and referral boundaries.
