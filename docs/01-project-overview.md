# 1. Project Overview

## Project title
**SMITAH — Crafting A Glimpse of Smile**

## Concept
SMITAH is a proposed creative well-being platform. It brings together personal identity, creative activities, mindfulness, skill development, virtual experiences and community participation in one planned user experience.

## Background
Digital experiences can provide more than information and entertainment. A thoughtfully designed platform may offer structured opportunities for reflection, creativity, learning and positive social interaction. SMITAH explores this design direction.

The proposal does not claim that an application can guarantee happiness or treat mental-health conditions. It explores how accessible creative and reflective activities could form part of everyday well-being routines.

## Vision
To propose an inclusive and personalised digital space where people can explore creativity, discover interests and connect through positive shared experiences.

## Mission
To organise creative prompts, mindfulness activities, skill-building challenges, virtual exploration and community interaction into a coherent user-centred concept.

## Design principles
- Personalisation and user choice.
- Creative expression and exploration.
- Optional participation without pressure.
- Respectful community interaction.
- Reflection and continuous refinement.
- Privacy, accessibility and responsible well-being boundaries.
