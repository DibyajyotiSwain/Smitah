# 6. Approach and Methodology

The original approach diagram is a continuous cycle. The following explanation expands its stages while retaining the original intent.

## Stage 1 — Research and User Persona
Explore intended users, interests, activity preferences, accessibility needs and privacy concerns. Develop illustrative personas and validate assumptions through suitable research.

**Output:** user-needs summary and persona profiles.

## Stage 2 — Personalised Quiz for User Preferences
Prepare an optional quiz about interests and preferred ways of participating. It is not a psychological test or diagnosis.

**Output:** preference categories and transparent mapping to suggested activity categories.

## Stage 3 — Diverse Customisation Options
Allow users to adjust profile details, interests, interface preferences and activity choices.

**Output:** personalisation rules and conceptual interface options.

## Stage 4 — Gamified Skill Forge Expeditions
Propose optional creative challenges that encourage exploration and practice. Rewards should recognise effort without creating pressure or guilt.

**Output:** sample challenge cards, progress concepts and reward principles.

## Stage 5 — Regular Updates Based on Insights
Review suggestions, usability findings and accessibility issues. Prioritise improvements and explain changes.

**Output:** feedback process and improvement backlog.

## Cycle
Research informs the quiz. Preferences inform customisation. Customisation shapes activities. Activities create opportunities for reflection and feedback. Feedback informs further research and refinement.
