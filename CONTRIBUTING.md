# Contribution Guidelines
- Preserve the four original SMITAH pillars.
- Do not describe conceptual features as implemented.
- Do not add statistics without traceable sources.
- Label fictional personas and sample quiz responses.
- Keep diagrams consistent with feature descriptions.
- Record research sources in `research/README.md`.
- Avoid collecting or publishing sensitive personal information.
- Treat healer interactions as a proposed concept requiring qualification, safeguarding and clear service boundaries.
